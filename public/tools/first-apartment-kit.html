02

Decide what earns its space.

This is a starter set of prompts, not a must-buy list. Choose an action for the items that matter to you. Leave the rest, skip them, or add your own.

Before buying: What job will it do? What do I already have that does that job? Where will it live? Can I try a shared or borrowed option first?

Choose the prompts that help you.

The tool supports up to 60 items. Fewer is fine. It is not a completion challenge.

03

Measure before bringing it home.

Use the same unit for all six dimensions in each check. Compare the space you can actually use, with room to walk, open doors, and operate the item.

A dimension check is not a fit guarantee. It does not calculate rotations, assembly, doorways, stairs, elevators, packaging, or manufacturer clearances. Check the full delivery route and installation instructions yourself.

Changing the unit label does not convert numbers. Re-enter measurements in the selected unit.

04

Make the move, one useful step at a time.

Suggested timing, not official deadlines. Adapt this to your lease, building, providers, household, and access needs. Start where you are, even when moving day is close.

05

Your plan should belong to you.

Keep a copy, take it offline, or share a snapshot with a roommate. There is no account to create and nothing to unlock.
